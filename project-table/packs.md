Bundled CMSIS device pack so the RA4M1 target resolves offline.

- **Responsibility:** Ship a trimmed `Renesas.RA_DFP` (source 6.4.0) that defines pyOCD's `r7fa4m1ab` target and its flash algorithms, so RA4M1 flashing works without `pyocd pack install` / a populated cmsis-pack-manager cache.
- **Key files:** `Renesas.RA_DFP.slim.pack`, `README.md`.
- **Relationships:** Bundled by `esp-prog2-flasher.spec` (`datas`); resolved by `flasher.find_default_pack()` and passed to pyOCD in `_open_session` (`options["pack"]`) for RA4M1 sessions only. PSoC 6 sessions use pyOCD's built-in `cy8c6xx5` target and skip the pack (`use_pack=False`).

- **`Renesas.RA_DFP.slim.pack`** — ~47 KB pack keeping only the `.pdsc` and the three RA4M1 flash algorithms (the full pack is ~88 MB, mostly SVDs), sized for the PyInstaller one-file build.
- **`README.md`** — Why the pack is slimmed and how to regenerate it from a full `RA_DFP` pack.
